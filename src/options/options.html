    Privacy

    The extension does not track you and does not collect your browsing history. To work out an estimate it sends calcbloc.com only the salary figure, the country, the region and the tax basics you set here (plus your API key, if you added one). It does not send the page address, the job title, the employer or anything else from the page. Your settings stay on this device.
